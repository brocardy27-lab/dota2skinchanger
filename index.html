Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

Yes. The core changer is free to download and use.

Can other players see my skins?

No. Changes are client-side only, so only you see them. Other players see your real equipped items.

Can I get banned?

Modifying game files may go against the game's Terms of Service. The tool only changes visuals and gives no gameplay advantage, but we can't guarantee how the publisher will treat it. Use at your own risk.

Does it work after a game update?

Updates can reset your files. Re-open the app after a patch. It'll update itself and re-apply your preset.

How do I remove everything?

Click Restore originals in the app, or verify game files through your game launcher.

My antivirus flagged the file. Why?

Tools that modify game files are sometimes flagged heuristically. Only download from this site and verify the SHA-256 checksum we publish with each release.
